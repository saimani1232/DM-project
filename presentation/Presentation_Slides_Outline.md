## Likely viva questions
* **Why is R² so high?** The dataset is nearly deterministic (σc = 1.25·σt exactly, bed temperature has no effect, few discrete levels). The value describes this dataset, not new prints. That is why we also report CV variance, the safety margins and the L15 discrepancy.
* **Why HGB rather than the paper's ANN?** It is the best on every metric under 5-fold CV, needs no feature scaling, and supports exact SHAP explanations through TreeExplainer.
* **Why a robust optimiser?** Tree models are piecewise constant. A plain optimiser puts the recipe on a step edge half-way between tested levels, where the model has no data. Scoring by the worst case within ±5 units keeps recipes on stable plateaus.
* **Why do all three objectives give the same optimum?** Because σc ≈ 1.25·σt in the data, maximising one maximises the other.
* **Is the simulator using the real model?** Yes. `build_simulator.py` exports the 600 trained trees, and `test_runtime.js` checks that the browser predictions equal Python's to within 10⁻¹⁰ MPa on 500 recipes.
* **How do we know the optimiser found the true optimum?** It is checked against an exhaustive search of all 960 tested combinations, and the pipeline's self-checks fail if it is worse.
