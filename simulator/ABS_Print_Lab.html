Virtual load test

The current recipe is printed into both parts and loaded until it fails. A part passes when its worst-case strength under ±5 units of process drift clears the requirement plus a model-error margin (2 × cross-validated RMSE).

Optimiser bench

Every grey dot is a recipe in the tested window, plotted by worst-case strength against print time. The red line is the Pareto front. Run differential evolution and watch its population converge, or click any dot to print that recipe.

0.2 mm recipes 0.8 mm recipes Pareto front Optimiser population Current recipe
Two populations of 24 recipes (one per layer height) evolve for 45 generations. Each recipe is scored by its worst case under ±5 units of process drift.
Press run to start the search.

Predictions between the tested levels (e.g. 35 mm/s or 96 % infill) are interpolations by the model. The robust worst case and the safety margin guard against that, but recipes should be confirmed with ASTM D638 / D695 test prints. The print-time index is relative: deposited volume (30 % shell plus the infilled core) divided by speed × layer height.

Reference: G.A. Munshi, V.M. Kulkarni, S. Yargatti, Next Materials 10 (2026) 101420. Course project, Introduction to Digital Manufacturing.
